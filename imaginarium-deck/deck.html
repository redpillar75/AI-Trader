Deep dive · 09

Imaginarium is not one course — it is three lenses on the same object, studied simultaneously until the object becomes visible.

The conceptual lens

We start with ideas — unmoored, unconditional, and untested. This lens asks: what is possible before constraints arrive?

  • Generative exercises — rapid-fire prompts designed to exhaust the obvious answers and reach the interesting ones.
  • Cross-domain borrowing — taking a structure from biology, a rhythm from music, a logic from architecture.
  • Failure catalogues — documenting every idea that didn't work and understanding why.
Weeks 1–4 · Pillar 01 / Imagine

The structural lens

Blueprint is where chaos becomes something someone else can read.

Structure doesn't kill imagination. It gives imagination somewhere to live. Every great world has an underlying grammar — rules that make surprise possible.

In this lens we study how blueprints work across disciplines: floor plans, story structures, circuit diagrams, choreography notation, musical scores. What do they have in common?

Weeks 5–8 · Pillar 02 / Blueprint

The material lens

  • Making under constraint — build something real with only what is in the room.
  • Iteration speed — three rough prototypes beat one polished draft every time.
  • The gap report — a written account of where the blueprint met reality and what had to change.
  • Public defense — every final build is presented to a crit panel including at least one person outside the course.

How every project
moves through
the five stages.

No project graduates to the next stage without a documented artifact from the current one. The output of each stage is the input of the next.
01 · Spark

Generate

Produce twenty ideas in one session. No filtering. Volume is the goal.

02 · Select

Choose one

Pick the idea that scares you most. Write one sentence explaining why.

03 · Map

Blueprint it

Sketch the structure. Name all the parts. Identify what you don't know yet.

04 · Make

Build fast

First version in two sessions. Rough is fine. Ship it to the crit table.

05 · Defend

Explain it

Present to the panel. Field every question. Update the blueprint to match reality.

Output

Artifact

A signed, documented work — blueprint and build, both submitted together.
